# User Story: 17 - Join a Community Challenge

**As a** runner,
**I want** to join a community running challenge,
**so that** I can pursue a shared goal with people who want the same kind of challenge.

## Acceptance Criteria

*   Available challenges can be browsed with their goal, duration and participant count.
*   A runner can join a challenge and see their contribution towards the challenge goal.
*   A challenge view shows participant standings and time remaining.
*   A runner can leave a challenge at any time.
*   Completing a challenge awards the associated badge.

## Notes

*   Transcript: "We can also have a community so that they can have communities that do what type of challenge they want to do."
*   Creating and moderating communities is deliberately out of scope for this story.
