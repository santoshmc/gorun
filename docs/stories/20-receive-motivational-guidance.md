# User Story: 20 - Get Motivational Nudges and Coaching

**As a** runner,
**I want** encouraging, mentor-style prompts about my plan and progress,
**so that** I stay inspired and build a lasting healthy habit.

## Acceptance Criteria

*   A reminder is sent ahead of a planned run day.
*   An encouraging message is shown after a completed run, referencing progress towards the weekly or monthly goal.
*   When a planned run is missed, the app suggests a realistic way to catch up rather than only reporting failure.
*   Notification frequency can be adjusted or turned off entirely.

## Notes

*   Transcript: "build an application which is inspiring for the person to keep his or her health in check... It's like a mentoring app."
