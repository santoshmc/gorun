# User Story: 13 - Earn Badges for Achievements

**As a** runner,
**I want** to earn badges when I complete milestones,
**so that** my effort feels rewarded and I keep coming back.

## Acceptance Criteria

*   Badges are awarded automatically for defined achievements such as completing a week's target, completing a monthly goal, and reaching streak milestones.
*   A notification is shown when a badge is earned.
*   A badge gallery shows earned badges with the date earned, and locked badges with the criteria needed to unlock them.
*   Badges are revoked if the underlying achievement is invalidated by a corrected activity.
