# User Story: 15 - Share My Runs and Streaks on Other Platforms

**As a** runner,
**I want** to share a completed run, streak or badge to platforms such as Strava, Facebook or LinkedIn,
**so that** I can celebrate progress with my wider network.

## Acceptance Criteria

*   A share action is available on a completed run, a badge and a streak milestone.
*   A share card is generated showing distance, pace, time and the achievement.
*   Sharing is supported to at least one external platform at MVP, plus a generic link/image share.
*   The runner previews and can edit the accompanying text before posting.
*   Nothing is shared without an explicit action by the runner.

## Notes

*   Transcript mentions Facebook, Strava, Garmin and LinkedIn as target networks.
