# User Story: 11 - Track My Monthly Goal Progress

**As a** runner,
**I want** to see how much of my monthly target I have completed and what remains,
**so that** I can adjust my effort to close the month successfully.

## Acceptance Criteria

*   A monthly progress view shows total distance run, percentage of target achieved and distance remaining.
*   Remaining distance is shown alongside remaining days and the daily average now required to finish on target.
*   At month end the goal is marked as achieved or missed.
*   Past months and their outcomes can be viewed in a history list.

## Notes

*   Transcript: "Successively, across the month, have they reached the 100 km mark or not?" and "I wanted to see if the monthly targets are closed or not, and then the reverse also."
