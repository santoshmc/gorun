# User Story: 2 - Generate a Systematic Weekly Run Plan from My Goal

**As a** runner,
**I want** the app to break my monthly target into a weekly and per-day run schedule,
**so that** I know exactly how far to run on each day instead of guessing.

## Acceptance Criteria

*   Given a monthly target, the app derives a weekly target (e.g. 100 km/month → ~25 km/week).
*   The runner chooses how many running days per week they want (e.g. 3, 4 or 5 days).
*   The app proposes a distribution of distance across the selected days and shows the resulting per-day distances.
*   At least two distribution styles are offered: even split (e.g. 5 km × 5 days) and progressive/long-run split (e.g. shorter mid-week runs plus one longer run).
*   Rest days are shown explicitly in the plan.
*   The generated plan covers every week of the goal month and is saved to the runner's schedule.

## Notes

*   Transcript: "Is it 5 km a day, or, say, 4 or 5 a day? 10 after 2 days: what's the best approach?"
